# Elecrow CrowPanel e-paper 4.2" (build `epd400`)

A 4.2" 400x300 e-paper with a dial and two keys, on an ESP32-S3.

- Build: `epd400`, `RADAR_DISPLAY` 5 (`DISPLAY_EPD_400x300`), target esp32s3. `RADAR_EPAPER` 1: slow refresh, 4 greys,
  no animation. The same number in OWLOS WiFi Radar.
- How sure: measured. It runs Flight Radar on the board on our desk since 2026-11-22 (4 greys, a partial print on top in
  about 1.2 s). Not in Flight Radar's CI, manifests or site yet.

## Chip, flash, PSRAM

ESP32-S3 N8R8 ([esp32-s3](../chips/esp32-s3.md)): 8 MB flash (read off the board, 2026-11-22), 8 MB octal PSRAM. Built
with the 4 MB layout since then: ViewOwl's 2 MB layout left the app 1.3 MB, and with the paper screens and their fonts
it is 1,443,696 bytes (built). Which USB path it has (a bridge or the S3's own) is not recorded.

## Screen

SSD1683 400x300, 4 greys, bit-banged SPI, panel power on GPIO 7 (has to be high), BUSY high = busy. Panel pins and
command sequences are ViewOwl's (ViewOwl.ESP32_EPD.Client). E-paper driver:
../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/epaper/ssd1683.md.

- Greys: ViewOwl's waveform gave 0, 50, 220, 255 (measured in ViewOwl), and the light grey was invisible on the glass.
  The grey group of the 4-grey LUT (its third, RP 5, frames to white then to black) went from 8:3 light and 8:5 dark to
  7:3 (byte 141 = 0x47) and 9:5 (byte 99 = 0x49): a photo of test bands showed 4 clear tones, read off as 95 and 185
  for the two greys (measured).
- Its screens are drawn for paper (core/ui_paper.c, the paper weather pages): see esp32-ui-drawing.
- A new screen is printed with a full refresh; on top, only partial prints of what changed.

## Keys

- Elecrow's 4.2" wiki and Mischianti's pinout give the 5.79"'s pins: dial up 6, down 4, press 5, MENU 2, BACK (EXIT)
  1, BOOT 0 (docs).
- On the board they sit on the right edge of the glass as drawn, top to bottom: a key, the dial, a key (a photo of the
  board). Which key is MENU and which EXIT is still a guess in the drawing: MENU top, EXIT bottom.
- board.h (2026-11-22) says the keys were not yet pressed here; later notes record a person pressing MENU on the board,
  and the serial console's `OWLOS KEY n` test hook pressing every key (measured).

## Pins

SCK 12, MOSI 11, RES 47, DC 46, CS 45, BUSY 48, power 7. Keys 6, 4, 5, 2, 1, BOOT 0. CS 45 and DC 46 are S3 strapping
pins, by the maker's design.

## board.h

```c
#elif RADAR_DISPLAY == DISPLAY_EPD_400x300 || RADAR_DISPLAY == DISPLAY_EPD_792x272
/* Elecrow CrowPanel e-paper 4.2" (400x300) and 5.79" (792x272), ESP32-S3 N8R8. panel pins and command
 * sequences are ViewOwl's (ViewOwl.ESP32_EPD.Client). the 4.2" runs this radar (2026-11-22, 4 greys,
 * drawn on top in 1.2 s); its keys are the same as the 5.79"'s in Elecrow's 4.2" wiki and in
 * Mischianti's pinout of it, not yet pressed here. the 5.79" ran the WiFi radar */
#define DISP_EPD       1
#define EPD_PIN_SCK   12
#define EPD_PIN_MOSI  11
#define EPD_PIN_RES   47
#define EPD_PIN_DC    46
#define EPD_PIN_CS    45
#define EPD_PIN_BUSY  48
#define EPD_PIN_PWR    7        // panel power, has to be high
#define DISP_STRIP     8        // 792 x 24 rows x 4 bytes was 76 KB in one piece, not there after wifi. paper is slow anyway
#define PIN_KEY_UP     6        // dial up
#define PIN_KEY_DOWN   4        // dial down
#define PIN_KEY_OK     5        // dial pressed
#define PIN_KEY_MENU   2
#define PIN_KEY_BACK   1
#define PIN_BUTTON     0
```

## sdkconfig and partitions

`sdkconfig.defaults` + `.esp32s3`: 4 MB, `partitions.csv`, CPU 240, octal PSRAM by name (`IGNORE_NOTFOUND`,
`USE_CAPS_ALLOC`). Flash at the default 40 MHz, DIO (`flash_args`, built). Copies in ../board-files.md.

## Console test hooks (DISP_EPD builds)

`OWLOS KEY n` presses a key; `OWLOS EPDLUT i hexv` changes one LUT byte in RAM (lost on reset, no value puts all back);
`OWLOS EPDTEST` holds 6 grey bands for 10 minutes. Keys sent during a 6 s refresh overwrite each other.

## Traps

- The disc map on the RADAR look is 2 x 118 = 236 px wide and the map keeps rows as words of 8 texels: the last 4
  texels wrote past each band and RADAR -> MAP crashed (heap). Fixed by rounding the disc up to 8 and refusing a width
  that does not divide by 8. Only this board had it.
- Without WiFi the sheet is blank with "MAP NEEDS WIFI": demo sky, no tiles.
