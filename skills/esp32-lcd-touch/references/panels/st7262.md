# ST7262 (RGB parallel 800x480)

The 800x480 panels on the ESP32-S3 RGB boards. An RGB-interface TFT driver: the S3 sends no start-up commands,
no MADCTL and no window; the panel shows what the S3's LCD peripheral clocks out of a frame in PSRAM. All the
work is on the bus side: [bus-rgb](../bus-rgb.md). Sureness words as in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md).

## Where it is

- Elecrow CrowPanel Advance 5.0 (IPS) [measured on a tester's V1.3: picture and touch work].
- Guition JC8048W550C [built: the controller from a shop's listing and BOARDS-WANTED; the pins from one board
  file].
- Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-7 (the -7, 800x480), named by chipguyhere's library [measured by its
  contributor].
- Not ported: Sunton ESP32-8048S043, S050, S070.

## What a board has to give

- The pixel clock and the six porch numbers (pulse, back, front, for H and V), and the polarities: every board
  here samples on the falling clock edge (`pclk_active_neg`); the plain CrowPanel 5.0 wants both syncs idle low.
  The numbers per board are in [bus-rgb](../bus-rgb.md).
- The data lines in order, blue lowest. Sources disagree on which five are red: keep both orders as screen
  profiles (a pin swap, not a MADCTL bit).
- Where its backlight and reset are: a plain GPIO, an I2C expander, or a small microcontroller.

## What it does not need

- No inversion, no gamma, no sleep out from the S3. There is nothing to `remind()`.
- No flip command: the frame is written turned over instead.
