# ST7121 (the M5Stack Tab5's panel, board V1.0)

One of the three 720x1280 MIPI-DSI panels the Tab5 is sold with: ILI9881C with a GT911 touch, ST7123, and ST7121
with its own touch. Sureness words as in [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md).

- The firmware never talks to the panel itself: Espressif's board package `espressif/m5stack_tab5` tells the
  three apart at power on and brings up the one it finds, with its touch. How the firmware sits on it:
  [bus-mipi-dsi](../bus-mipi-dsi.md).
- The ST7121 is the one seen: OWLOS Flight Radar runs on a contributor's Tab5 with it (board V1.0): picture,
  map and touch [measured, a contributor's board]. Not run here.
- The ILI9881C and ST7123 versions: not seen anywhere here. To learn from anyone with one: which panel it has,
  does the flash from the site go through, does the WiFi join.
- Board file: [tab5](../../../esp32-boards/references/boards/tab5.md).
