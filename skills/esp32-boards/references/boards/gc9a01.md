# Elecrow CrowPanel 1.28" round (build `gc9a01`)

A round 240x240 touch screen on an ESP32-C3, with reset and backlight behind an I2C expander.

- Build: `gc9a01`, `RADAR_DISPLAY` 4 (`DISPLAY_GC9A01_240x240`), target esp32c3. `RADAR_ROUND` 1: the glass is a
  circle, nothing may sit in the corners.
- How sure: measured, on the board on our desk (ViewOwl's C3 build first, then both radars).

## Chip, flash, USB

- ESP32-C3 ([esp32-c3](../chips/esp32-c3.md)), one core at 160 MHz, 4 MB flash on the one here (measured), no PSRAM.
  Built for 2 MB with `partitions_2mb.csv`: the comment in `sdkconfig.defaults.esp32c3` says 2 MB, the chip has 4.
- USB-C. The notes disagree on what is behind it: Flight Radar's say the desk's boards are all CH340 and "UART console
  fine" on this one, the ESP32 Web Flasher's say "C3 native USB". Elecrow's wiki speaks of a UART serial port and puts
  the encoder on GPIO 18 and 19, the C3's USB pins, so a bridge is the likely truth (reasoned). The schematic zip on the
  wiki settles it, not opened yet. On the desk the port list's VID settles it (1A86 or 303A).

## Screen

GC9A01 240x240 round, MADCTL 0x68 (0x28 came out mirrored), inversion on, SPI 80 MHz on SCLK 6, MOSI 7, CS 10, DC 2,
no MISO. Snow on the screen would mean go to 40. Measured. Strips of 16 rows: the heap is small, room for the map and
TLS (24 in WiFi Radar). Panel: ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/panels/gc9a01.md.

Reset and backlight sit on a PI4IOE5V6408 I2C expander at 0x43 on SDA 4, SCL 5, not on GPIOs: backlight bit 2, touch
reset bit 3, LCD reset bit 4 (measured, the sequence in ../board-code.md). The backlight has no PWM there, on or off:
no BACKLIGHT setting on this board. Elecrow's wiki adds a vibration motor on the expander's P0 (docs, unconfirmed).

## Touch

Capacitive at 0x15 on the same I2C. CST816 was a guess and it answered; a shop page says CST816D (docs). It dozes off
after a few idle seconds and then ignores I2C until touched: register 0xFE = 1 keeps it awake (measured). The 5 cross
alignment finds how its axes sit, it came out at 10 px (measured). Span 240. touch.c goes on without touch if nothing
answers. ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/touch/cst816.md.

## Buttons and other parts

- BOOT 9.
- Elecrow's wiki: a custom button on 1, a rotary encoder A 19, B 18, press 8, a buzzer on 3, a BM8563 RTC on the I2C,
  a battery socket (docs, none used).

## board.h

```c
#elif RADAR_DISPLAY == DISPLAY_GC9A01_240x240
/* Elecrow CrowPanel 1.28" round, ESP32-C3 (4 MB on the one here). ViewOwl's C3 build.
 * runs (2026-10-13): ~8 fps, 31 KB heap left. reset and backlight are on a PI4IOE5V6408
 * I2C expander, not on GPIOs. the touch chip is not named anywhere in ViewOwl, CST816 at
 * 0x15 was a guess and it answered; touch.c still goes on without touch if nothing does */
#define DISP_LCD_SPI   1
#define LCD_CHIP_GC9A01 1
#define PIN_LCD_SCLK   6
#define PIN_LCD_MOSI   7
#define PIN_LCD_MISO  (-1)
#define PIN_LCD_DC     2
#define PIN_LCD_CS    10
#define PIN_LCD_RST   (-1)      // on the expander
#define LCD_SPI_HZ    (80 * 1000 * 1000)   // C3 IOMUX pins, ViewOwl runs 80. snow on screen = go to 40
#define LCD_MADCTL    0x68      // MV|MX|BGR, ViewOwl's lcd_gc9a01.c (0x28 came out mirrored)
#define DISP_STRIP    16        // 15 strips: the C3's heap is small, room for the map and TLS
#define PIN_I2C_SDA    4
#define PIN_I2C_SCL    5
#define IOEX_ADDR      0x43
#define IOEX_BIT_BL    (1 << 2)
#define IOEX_BIT_TRST  (1 << 3)  // touch reset
#define IOEX_BIT_RST   (1 << 4)  // LCD reset
#define TOUCH_CST816   1
#define CST816_ADDR    0x15
#define PIN_BUTTON     9        // BOOT on the C3
```

## sdkconfig and partitions

`sdkconfig.defaults` + `.esp32c3`: CPU 160, 2 MB, `partitions_2mb.csv` (app 0x150000, tiles 0xA0000 at 0x160000), QIO
80. The app is 1,308,416 bytes, 95% of its slot (built, 2026-11-19).

## Measured

- WiFi Radar: 8 fps, 31 KB heap. It heard fewer networks (10) than the ESP32 boards.
- Flight Radar: 154 to 161 ms a frame with every radar option on, least free heap 26 KB (0.6); weather dial 6 to 7 fps
  after the integer disc, graphs 7 to 9; 5 to 6 fps with 48 planes (0.16.2). Heap at fetch time about 57 KB (0.14).
- On a bench at -92 dBm: 34 of 52 tiles came broken, 7 WiFi drops; about 9 joins in 10 ended in reason 201 there.

## Traps

- One core: a fetch task below the screen's priority starved IDLE and the watchdog reset the board every ~29 s.
  [esp32-c3](../chips/esp32-c3.md).
- The app slot is nearly full.
- Its home country (`cc`) is RAM only, asked from Nominatim at start; after several quick restarts Nominatim refuses
  and `cc` stays empty for 5 to 10 minutes. A settings diff showing only `cc` right after flashing is that.
