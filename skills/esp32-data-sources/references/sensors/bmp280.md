# BMP280 (and BME280): the barometer that never spoke

Bosch's pressure sensor. The OWLOS Air Quality board has one on I2C, at 0x76 or 0x77. From `code/aq_sensor_hw.c`; the board and
the pressure model (tendency, Zambretti) in `../air-quality.md`.

How sure: silent at 0x76 and 0x77 on all 7 boards. [measured] The cause is open. The start and read below are written to the
datasheet and never ran against a chip here. [built]

## What Bosch's datasheet says, and the screen says

- If CSB is ever low, I2C stays off until the power is cut. Check CSB at about 3.3 V, VDD under 3.6 V. [docs]
- Not resolved on these boards.

## The firmware does not wait for it to be solved

- A BMP280 or BME280 module on SDA 21, SCL 22, 3.3 V and GND is found within 10 seconds, at 0x76 or 0x77, by id 0x58 or 0x60.
- With no barometer the METEO page says "No barometer answers", what to check and what to plug in.

```c
// after the id: reset 0xE0 = 0xB6, 10 ms, read 24 bytes of calibration from 0x88
i2c_wr(s_bmp, 0xF5, (const uint8_t[]){ 0x90 }, 1);    // standby 500 ms, IIR filter x16
i2c_wr(s_bmp, 0xF4, (const uint8_t[]){ 0x57 }, 1);    // T x2, P x16, normal mode
// reading: 6 bytes from 0xF7, the datasheet's 64 bit integer compensation, Q24.8 Pa, / 256 / 100 = hPa
```

So everything about pressure on that board (the 3-hour tendency, the alarm, the Zambretti forecast) has run only on the made-up
pressure of `core/sim.c` and on the preview. [PC test] Say so.

## Traps

- No pressure on any board: the BMP280 silent. Say so on screen, offer a module, retry every 10 s. [measured, cause open]
