# 3.5" CYD, ESP32-3248S035 R and C (build `st7796`)

Sunton's 3.5" yellow board, sold as R (resistive touch) and C (capacitive). One build covers both and LCDWIKI's
E32R35T.

- Build: `st7796`, `RADAR_DISPLAY` 2 (`DISPLAY_ST7796_480x320`), target esp32. ViewOwl's 480x320 build originally.
- How sure: every number measured "on the 3.5"" here was measured on an LCDWIKI E32R35T ([e32r35t](e32r35t.md)), and
  from 2026-11-20 on a second 3.5" unit whose maker the notes do not record. A real Sunton has the same pins by
  rzeldent's JSON; the only difference found is the LED's red. The notes never saw a real Sunton. The C version's
  GT911 path is built: the probe and the bus being given back are proven on the R path with no GT911 present, the
  GT911 reads are not.

## Chip, flash, USB

Classic ESP32 ([esp32](../chips/esp32.md)), 4 MB, no PSRAM (docs). USB: a bridge to UART0.

## Screen

ST7796 480x320 SPI, BGR, MADCTL 0xE8, no inversion, 40 MHz, BL 27 (PWM). Panel:
../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/panels/st7796.md.

- Strips of 6 rows since 2026-11-18 (8 before): with the weather the tile reader had too little room on the square
  map; each row less is 3.8 KB less for the strip and its DMA buffers, and as much less for the second core. Cost:
  MAP 12 -> 10 fps, CLASSIC 11 -> 9, RADAR the same (measured).
- The second core (`DISP_HELPER`) draws every other strip in the radar and weather looks and their pages; the square
  maps stay on one core: their picture wants the memory, and there the SPI is what a frame waits for.
- The screen is told its state again every 5 s, between frames (`remind()` in lcd_spi.c), after an E32R35T was found
  standing still after about a day.

## Touch

- R: XPT2046 on the LCD bus, CS 33, IRQ 36, at 2 MHz (`TOUCH_XPT_SPI`). Before a touch transfer the driver waits for
  the strip in flight. ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/touch/xpt2046.md.
- C: a capacitive GT911 on I2C SDA 33, SCL 32, RST 25, INT 21 (rzeldent, docs), at 0x5D or 0x14 by the INT level at
  power on. The same build asks for a GT911 first (`TOUCH_GT911_TOO`): reads "911" at register 0x8140 and, when nobody
  answers, frees the I2C bus, since 33 is the XPT2046's CS on the R. It leaves RST and INT alone. GT911 counts screen
  pixels, 320x480 standing. ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/touch/gt911.md.

## Buttons, LEDs, other parts

BOOT 0. RGB LED 4, 16, 17, low = lit (red 22 on the E32R35T). Speaker 26, light sensor 34, SD 5, 23, 18, 19
(rzeldent's JSON, docs).

## Pins

LCD bus SCLK 14, MOSI 13, MISO 12 (only the touch reads it), DC 2, CS 15, reset none, BL 27. Touch CS 33, IRQ 36. C
version I2C SDA 33, SCL 32.

## board.h

```c
#elif RADAR_DISPLAY == DISPLAY_ST7796_480x320
/* ESP32-3248S035 (3.5" ST7796), ViewOwl's 480x320 build. runs (2026-10-13): ~8 fps, 24 KB
 * heap left. the touch is the R version, XPT2046 on the LCD bus (CS 33), works */
#define DISP_LCD_SPI   1
#define LCD_CHIP_ST7796 1
#define PIN_LCD_SCLK  14
#define PIN_LCD_MOSI  13
#define PIN_LCD_MISO  12        // only the touch reads it
#define PIN_LCD_DC     2
#define PIN_LCD_CS    15
#define PIN_LCD_RST   (-1)
#define PIN_LCD_BL    27
#define LCD_SPI_HZ    (40 * 1000 * 1000)
#define LCD_MADCTL    0xE8      // MY|MX|MV|BGR, ViewOwl lcd_init.c
/* 480 wide and no PSRAM: low strips, the map's picture needs the heap. 6, not 8, since the weather
 * came (2026-11-18): 8 left the tile reader too little room on the square map after a while, and
 * each row less is 3.8 KB less for the strip and its DMA buffers, and as much less for the second core */
#define DISP_STRIP    6
/* twice the 2.8"'s pixels on the same chip: in the radar and the weather looks, and on their pages,
 * the second core draws every other strip (main.c). the square maps leave it be: their picture wants
 * the memory, and there the SPI is what a frame waits for */
#define DISP_HELPER   1
#define TOUCH_XPT_SPI 1
#define PIN_T_CS      33
#define PIN_T_IRQ     36
#define PIN_BUTTON     0
/* the C version (ESP32-3248S035C) has the same screen and a capacitive GT911 on I2C instead,
 * SDA 33 SCL 32 (rzeldent/platformio-espressif32-sunton). touch.c asks for it first and gives
 * the pins back when nobody answers: on the R version 33 is the XPT2046's chip select.
 * NOT run on a C version here */
#define TOUCH_GT911_TOO 1
#define PIN_I2C_SDA   33
#define PIN_I2C_SCL   32
```

The comment calls the board ESP32-3248S035; the one measured is an E32R35T.
