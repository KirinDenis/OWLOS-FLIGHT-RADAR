# Elecrow CrowPanel e-paper 5.79" (build `epd792`)

A wide 792x272 e-paper made of two SSD1683 halves, with a dial and two side keys, on an ESP32-S3.

- Build: `epd792`, `RADAR_DISPLAY` 6 (`DISPLAY_EPD_792x272`), target esp32s3. `RADAR_EPAPER` 1. The same number in
  OWLOS WiFi Radar.
- How sure: measured under OWLOS WiFi Radar on the board on our desk (WiFi Radar's board.h line "NOT run on hardware"
  is stale). Built under Flight Radar (app 1,276,944 bytes), not run there, not in its CI. It is not on the bench now.

## Chip, flash, PSRAM

ESP32-S3R8 ([esp32-s3](../chips/esp32-s3.md)), 8 MB octal PSRAM, 8 MB flash (measured). Built with the 4 MB layout.
PSRAM holds two 54 KB pictures and the 2-bit model of the glass, used by name.

## Screen

Two SSD1683 in a cascade, master and slave, 792x272, 4 greys measured 0, 60, 140, 255. Full 4-grey refresh 3.1 to
3.3 s, partial 1.86 s (measured). Pins as the 4.2" ([epd400](epd400.md)). E-paper driver:
../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/epaper/ssd1683.md.

- Strips of 8 rows: 792 x 24 rows x 4 bytes = 76 KB in one piece was not free after WiFi.
- Flight Radar draws no map on it: the map's widest is 480 (`MAP_W_MAX`), under 792. The paper weather pages are for
  the 4.2" only; this board keeps the colour pages printed in greys.
- The one here has a cracked right part of the glass: about 2 cm, about 114 px, so about 672 of 792 px still show. A
  photo-only estimate gave 648: perspective squeezes the far edge, the ruler won. Draw full width regardless (decided:
  a 672 px variant was built and removed).

## Keys

Two side keys, MENU GPIO 2 and BACK GPIO 1, and the dial on 6, 4, 5: up, down, press (measured; Elecrow's key
example). BOOT 0.

## board.h

The block is shared with the 4.2": see [epd400](epd400.md).

## sdkconfig and partitions

As [epd400](epd400.md): `sdkconfig.defaults` + `.esp32s3`, 4 MB, `partitions.csv`.

## Traps

After flashing it prints nothing until a reset: toggle RTS on an open port to see the boot (measured).
