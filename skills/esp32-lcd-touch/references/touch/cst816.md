# CST816 (capacitive)

Capacitive touch on I2C at 0x15. Read with the same code as the FT6336: [ft6336](ft6336.md). Sureness words as in
[SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md).

## Where it is

- Elecrow CrowPanel 1.28" round, ESP32-C3 [measured: alignment within 10 px]. The chip was not named anywhere in
  the sources; CST816 at 0x15 was a guess, and it answered. `tp_init` still goes on without touch if nothing
  does. Board file: [gc9a01](../../../esp32-boards/references/boards/gc9a01.md).
- Not ported, the same family: Guition JC2432W328C (CST816S or CST820 at 0x15), ESP32-2432S024C (CST820 at
  0x15; rzeldent's macro calls it CST816S, and its README says the CST820 is upward compatible),
  ESP32-2424S012C (CST816S).

## The facts

- **It dozes off** after a few idle seconds and then ignores I2C until touched. Write register 0xFE = 1 once at
  start and it stays awake [measured].
- Its reset is on the round board's I2C expander, released in `disp_init` ([gc9a01](../panels/gc9a01.md)): wait
  50 ms after that before asking.
- The first finger: 5 bytes from register 0x02, count (`& 0x0F`), X high and low, Y high and low (`& 0x0F` on
  the high bytes).
- `TOUCH_RAW_SPAN` 240.
- How the chip's axes sit on the round glass was not assumed: the alignment found it [measured].
- The C3's `i2c_master` with this chip and the expander has shown no hang [measured].
