# Guition JC8012P4A1C, 10.1" (build `jc8012`)

A 10.1" 800x1280 panel standing on 2-lane MIPI-DSI, a GSL3680 touch, an ESP32-P4 with an ESP32-C6 for the WiFi. Driven
by chipguyhere's library, the picture lying as 1280x800, with the moving layer put on by columns.

- Build: `jc8012`, `RADAR_DISPLAY` 17 (`DISPLAY_JC8012_1280x800`), target esp32p4. `RADAR_OVERLAY` 1,
  `RADAR_OVERLAY_COLS` 1. Also in build.sh. Released in 0.14.
- How sure: brought in by chipguyhere, who runs it on one (written and measured 2026-11-13 on a board with a v1.3 P4).
  Every number below is chipguyhere's (FORK-NOTES.md). Not run here. Experimental on the site.

## Chip, flash, PSRAM, USB

- ESP32-P4 ([esp32-p4](../chips/esp32-p4.md)), v1.3: 16 MB flash, 32 MB PSRAM.
- WiFi on the C6 over SDIO slot 1 as on Espressif's ESP32-P4-Function-EV-Board: CLK 18, CMD 19, D0 to D3 14 to 17, the
  C6's reset on 54 (`CONFIG_ESP_HOSTED_P4_DEV_BOARD_FUNC_BOARD`). The C6's own firmware 2.1.10 logs "major version
  mismatch" against ESP-Hosted 3.1.0; nothing failed so far.
- The console on UART0 through the board's CH340, its USB_UART port: the one to upload and watch on.
- Flash runs at 40 MHz on this build although asked for 80: not looked into.

## Screen

800x1280 JD9365 standing on 2-lane MIPI-DSI; the picture lies, 1280x800. chipguyhere's JC8012P4A1C display (1.0.0) and
board libraries, vendored in `components/chipguy_jc8012` with the 7/7B shim plus `analogWrite`, `getCpuFrequencyMhz`,
`rgbLedWrite`, own the panel, the backlight, the touch and the I2C bus (GPIO 7 and 8). The panel drivers
`esp_lcd_jd9365` (Espressif's) and `esp_lcd_panel_dpi_bb` (Espressif's, changed by chipguyhere) are Apache-2.0. Panel:
../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/panels/jd9365.md, bus ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/bus-mipi-dsi.md.

- The layout is the 5" boards' 5/2 as big: 512 x 5/2 is 1280 exactly. The fonts are the 3/2 ones: the writing is 3/5 of
  its size.
- Background strips of 80 rows drawn in PSRAM, turned into the standing frame (row y becomes the panel's column 799 - y).
  The panel's rows are the picture's columns: 80 chunks of 16 panel rows. A chunk the moving layer touches is copied
  into a bounce buffer and composited; the rest are read straight from the frame.
- Normal is camera up: `disp_flip` inverts the setting's sense on this board.

## Touch

GSL3680, through the library's MIT driver (`TOUCH_JC8012`): ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/touch/gsl3680.md. The controller runs from RAM and needs about 18 KB of
firmware uploaded at every power-up. That firmware is Silead's and GPL-2.0, so it is not in the MIT tree: the driver
reads it from NVS (namespace `jc8012`, key `gsl3680fw`), checks its SHA-256 and uploads it. It is put there once per
board by the `InstallFirmware` example of chipguyhere's Arduino library chipguy_ESP32-P4-C6-firmware-installer.
Without it the radar runs with the touch off and the log says `no touch: touch firmware not installed in NVS`.

- Order: the installer first, then this firmware. The installer's partition table puts NVS at 0x9000 to 0xDFFF (20 KB)
  and otadata at 0xE000; `partitions_jc8012.csv` has NVS at 0x9000 to 0xEFFF (24 KB). After the installer,
  `idf.py flash` of this build kept the blob: NVS accepted the Arduino otadata in its sixth page without the "no free
  pages" error that would make app_main erase it (measured 2026-11-15).
- Erasing the board from the site takes the touch firmware too (the site's `nvsfw` note on the erase box).
- Touch works: tapping a plane, turning the ring, the buttons (2026-11-13). The flip and BOOT not tried yet.

## Buttons

BOOT, GPIO 35, pulled up on the board: a button the firmware reads.

## board.h

```c
#elif RADAR_DISPLAY == DISPLAY_JC8012_1280x800
/* Guition JC8012P4A1C, 10.1": ESP32-P4 (16 MB flash, 32 MB PSRAM), an 800x1280 JD9365 panel standing on
 * 2-lane MIPI-DSI, a GSL3680 touch, Wi-Fi through an ESP32-C6 on SDIO (sdkconfig.defaults.jc8012). added by
 * chipguyhere, driven by chipguyhere's JC8012P4A1C library, vendored in components/chipguy_jc8012, which
 * owns the panel, the backlight, the touch and the I2C bus (GPIO 7 / 8). lcd_jc8012.c. the background
 * is turned into the standing frame as it is drawn (the PPA); the panel's rows are the picture's
 * columns, and those the moving layer touches are fed through two bounce buffers by the library's
 * raster band, with the layer put on as they are filled (ovl.c by columns). the rest are read from the
 * frame. BOOT (GPIO 35) is a button */
#define DISP_JC8012    1
#define DISP_STRIP    80        // rows a strip of the background (main.c bg_task), drawn in the PSRAM (no such room in the
                                // RAM, and the background has no deadline). tall, so each PPA turn writes 160 bytes a
                                // panel row: 8-row strips (16 bytes a row, 100 turns a frame) made the fill's fetches slow.
                                // a PPA turn (measured at boot, 2026-11-13): ~12 ms at 16 rows, ~27 at 80, ~42 at 128, then
                                // ~190 ms at 160 and ~925 ms for the whole picture: 80 is the cheapest a page (~270 ms)
#define TOUCH_JC8012   1
#define PIN_BUTTON    35        // BOOT, pulled up on the board
```

## sdkconfig and partitions

`sdkconfig.defaults` + `.esp32p4` (the Tab5's: PSRAM, clock, flash hold here too) + `.jc8012` (copy in
../board-files.md): the console on UART0, `partitions_jc8012.csv` (the 16 MB layout and a core dump on flash at
0xA10000), big state in PSRAM, plain malloc to PSRAM, no idle-task watchdog on core 0, the C6 on the function board's
wires, 128-byte L2 cache lines, XIP from PSRAM, run-time stats, and TLS, lwIP and ESP-Hosted's stacks in PSRAM.

## Measured (chipguyhere, 2026-11-13)

- Panel 52.0 Hz, 0 underruns. About 47 chunks a frame filled, about 32 straight from the frame. A filled chunk about
  103 us on core 1 of about 235. The background whole every ~0.41 s.
- Internal RAM: about 93 KB free in steady state, about 28 KB at the least (TLS); after the moves to PSRAM about
  133 KB free, 112 KB at the least.
- Chunks given up or late went to 0 with 80-row strips on a 32-plane test sky; the moving layer then changed about 4
  times a second there.

## Traps

- On a stock ESP-IDF 5.5.x the planner's PIE needs `OVL_PLANNER_PIE` 0 in ovl.c, or thin 8-pixel slivers appear: the
  FreeRTOS fix is in no 5.5.x release yet ([esp32-p4](../chips/esp32-p4.md)).
- The panel's first 3 chunks once showed their bounce buffers' leftovers for good: the raster band was still pending at
  start and the "read straight from the frame" bit was dropped. Fixed in the library (the bit kept always).
