# LCDWIKI E32R35T, 3.5" ESP32-32E (takes build `st7796`)

LCDWIKI's 3.5" ESP32-32E display with resistive touch, also sold by Elegoo. It looks like the Sunton 3.5" CYD and has
its screen and touch pins.

- Build: none of its own in Flight Radar, it takes `st7796` ([st7796](st7796.md)). FusionEdge has a build `e32r35t`.
- How sure: measured. The 3.5" board on the desk for weeks turned out to be one of these, found 2026-11-03: red lit
  with GPIO 22 alone held low.

## Chip, flash, USB

ESP32-32E module, classic ESP32 ([esp32](../chips/esp32.md)), 4 MB, no PSRAM. USB: a bridge to UART0 (measured).

## Screen and touch

- ST7796 320x480 IPS (LCDWIKI calls it IPS), on the HSPI pins: SCK 14, MOSI 13, MISO 12, CS 15, DC 2, backlight 27,
  reset by the ESP32's EN. No inversion. Measured under Flight Radar (MADCTL 0xE8) and FusionEdge (LovyanGFX rotation 3,
  the same way up as the 2.8").
- XPT2046 on the same bus: CS 33, IRQ 36, read through MISO 12. The pen IRQ keeps reads off the bus while nobody
  touches (measured).
- Panel and touch: ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/panels/st7796.md,
  ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/touch/xpt2046.md.

## LED, sound, other parts

- RGB LED, low = lit: red 22, green 16, blue 17 (measured: red on 22 alone).
- GPIO 4 enables the FM8002E amplifier, low = on. Nothing on the board tells it from a Sunton, so never drive both
  pins: red's pin is a setting (`led_red` 4 or 22, RED ON in SET's LED tab), ../board-code.md.
- Speaker on GPIO 26 (DAC channel 2). FusionEdge plays it at `AUDIO_DAC_LEVEL` 70: at the DAC's full swing this
  amplifier began to break up at volume 19 of 21 (measured by ear).
- microSD on VSPI: SCK 18, MISO 19, MOSI 23, CS 5. Battery voltage on 34.

## Measured (Flight Radar, build st7796)

- WiFi Radar: 8 fps, 118 ms a frame, 24 KB heap.
- MAP look 110 -> 81 ms after per-frame work moved out of the strips; SPI-bound, 61 ms of a frame is transfer at
  40 MHz. Later: 53 of 82 ms waiting on the bus.
- RADAR look 139 -> 98 ms: QIO 80 flash (124 -> 99 ms by its comment) and the second core drawing every other strip
  (about 1.25x, not 2x).
- 0.6 with every radar option on: 129 to 142 ms a frame with 18 planes.
- WEATHER: dial 8 to 10 fps, graphs 9 to 11; with the weather hud 9 to 12 fps, 80 to 106 ms a frame, heap 35 to 52 KB.
- MAP 10 fps and CLASSIC 9 with 6-row strips; the map outlines cost about 5 ms a frame (disc 45 -> 50 ms).
- Memory: the square map takes 77 KB, about 43 KB stays free, the tile reader about 38 KB. Before 2026-11-18, 4 of
  151 maps came empty ("no memory to read a tile in, heap 43440, largest 34816") and the least free heap was 880 B.
  After the 512 B window pieces and 6-row strips: 153 of 153 whole in a 20 minute cycle, 6.3 KB the least while a tile
  is read.
- Two hour log on 0.14: heap at fetch time about 44 KB, no leak, no restart.
- 0.13's map holes: OpenStreetMap sends sea and plain tiles as 1, 2 or 4 bit palette PNGs, which the decoder refused;
  fixed in 0.14.
- 10 of 10 restarts kept the look after the phantom tap fix (0.16.1): one noisy XPT2046 read had become a press.
- FusionEdge: MP3 streams play with about 50 KB in one piece, the touch works, the WiFi list can be written over the
  cable, the radio's web page serves 54 of 54 files and loads in about 1.2 s. AAC and HTTPS MP3 streams do not play:
  the decoder cannot open in the memory left.

## Traps

- One was found standing still after about a day, cause unknown. Since then the screen is told its state every 5 s.
- Twice it booted into RADAR after a flash though GET had said WEATHER; a plain restart booted WEATHER. Cause not
  found: read GET `look` first.
