# LCDWIKI E32R28T, 2.8" ESP32-32E (takes build `ili9341`)

LCDWIKI's 2.8" ESP32-32E display, also sold by Elegoo, on Amazon as "ESP32 ILI9341". A yellow board with USB-C, a BAT
socket and "ESP32-32E" printed on the back. It has the 2.8" CYD's screen and touch pins, red LED on another pin, and
an amplifier switched by GPIO 4.

- Build: none of its own. It takes Flight Radar's `ili9341` ([ili9341](ili9341.md)) and FusionEdge's `cyd28`.
- How sure: measured. The desk's two 2.8" boards were long called CYDs; a photo in November showed them to be
  E32R28T. The notes name no other 2.8" board on the desk, so every 2.8" measurement here is most likely from these
  (reasoned).

## Chip, flash, USB

- Classic ESP32 ([esp32](../chips/esp32.md)), ESP32-D0WD-V3, 4 MB, no PSRAM (measured).
- USB-C through a CH340 to UART0 (measured).

## Screen

ILI9341V, CS 15, DC 2, SCLK 14, MOSI 13, backlight 21, reset on EN (lcdwiki.com/2.8inch_ESP32-32E_Display, docs). On
the radar's v1 start-up (ILI9341, BGR, MADCTL 0xE8, no inversion): `SET screen 0`, measured. FusionEdge runs it with
LovyanGFX, inversion off, 40 MHz (measured). Panel: ../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/panels/ili9341.md.

## Touch

XPT2046 on its own pins: CLK 25, MOSI 32, MISO 39, CS 33, IRQ 36 (docs, and measured under both firmwares).
../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/touch/xpt2046.md.

## LED and sound

- RGB LED, low = lit: red 22 by LCDWIKI's page, green 16, blue 17 (docs).
- GPIO 4 enables the FM8002E speaker amplifier, low = on (docs, and measured: in FusionEdge a build holding GPIO 4 low
  made the silent speaker play).
- Not settled: the radar's notes keep the desk's 2.8" boards on `led_red` 4, set so after someone looked at the board,
  and the Thermo Relay lit "red on 4" on a 2.8" board. That was before the boards were known as E32R28T. Never drive
  both pins. To settle it: hold 22 low alone for minutes and ask whether red is lit. A model cannot see an LED; a person
  checks.
- The amplifier has about 10 dB more gain than the E32R35T's: FusionEdge played clean to volume 10 of 21 at the DAC's
  full swing and broke up from 11, so its `AUDIO_DAC_LEVEL` is 28 (percent of the DAC's swing) here against 70 on the
  3.5" (measured by ear).

## Measured

- WiFi Radar: 15 to 19 fps, a 2 hour log clean, 0 lost strips, 49 KB heap.
- Flight Radar, at 200 km with 48 planes, with the second core on half of each strip: RADAR 12 fps, WEATHER 17, MAP
  19, CLASSIC 15 (9, 12, 16, 13 before). WEATHER's dial 17 to 20 fps, its graphs 20 to 25. 19 to 21 fps with 10 planes
  (0.16.2). Heap at fetch time about 68 KB (0.14); with the weather down to about 25 KB during fetches; 17.9 KB the
  least while a tile is read.
- 0.6 with every radar option on: 77 to 79 ms a frame, least free heap 37 KB.
- 150 of 150 maps whole with looks and ranges switched every 5 s; a two hour log with no leak.
- A weak WiFi spot (-84 dBm): 3 of 112 tiles came broken; idle, every map whole.
- FusionEdge (no PSRAM, its lite screen): MP3 streams play with about 50 KB in one piece, the touch works. One board's
  USB went dead until the cable was flipped (a flaky cable, it died again).
- The speaker was silent on both boards until GPIO 4 was held low; a 500 Hz full-swing beep on 26 was silent too, and
  GPIO 26's registers read the same as on the playing 3.5": the amplifier's enable, not the firmware.

## Traps

- On a real CYD GPIO 4 is the red LED: a firmware that drives 4 for the amplifier lights red there (FusionEdge's
  `cyd28` build does, on purpose: one build for both).
- A board that ran FusionEdge carries an Arduino partition table (nvs at 0x9000 size 0x5000, otadata at 0xE000). The
  desk's boards went back to the radar from whole 4 MB backups read off before (`read_flash 0 ALL`), checked by asking
  `OWLOS HELLO` and comparing every setting field before and after (measured). From the site, flash with erase.
