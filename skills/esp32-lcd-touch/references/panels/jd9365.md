# JD9365 (MIPI-DSI 800x1280)

The 10.1" panel of the Guition JC8012P4A1C (ESP32-P4), standing 800x1280, driven by chipguyhere's library with
Espressif's `esp_lcd_jd9365` (Apache-2.0) for the vendor table. Not run here; it runs on its contributor's board.
Everything below is [docs, the library's source] unless it says measured. Sureness words as in
[SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md). How the firmware feeds it: [bus-mipi-dsi](../bus-mipi-dsi.md). Board file:
[jc8012](../../../esp32-boards/references/boards/jc8012.md).

## Bring-up, in order

1. Panel reset asserted first and held through the whole host-side bring-up, at least 50 ms.
2. The D-PHY's supply: the P4's on-chip LDO channel 3 at 2500 mV.
3. Backlight GPIO low.
4. DSI bus: 2 data lanes, 1500 Mbps a lane, PHY clock from PLL_F20M.
5. DBI panel IO for the commands, then the DPI panel: 60 MHz DPI clock, RGB565, H porches 20/20/40 and V
   4/8/20 (pulse, back, front), two framebuffers in PSRAM on the 128-byte cache line.
6. Reset released, 200 ms to settle.
7. ID read with 0x04 (3 bytes, for the log).
8. 0xE0 0x00, MADCTL 0x36 0xC0 (MY|MX: the panel's base scan; turning the picture is the PPA's work), 0x3A
   0x55, 0x80 0x01.
9. The vendor table from `esp_lcd_jd9365.c`, each command with its delay.
10. `esp_lcd_panel_init`: the DMA and video mode start.
11. 20 ms, then 0x11 sleep out, 120 ms, 0x29 display on, 20 ms: after video mode, not before.
12. Both frames cleared and written back from the cache.

## Recovery

- A watchdog reset restarts the chip but never power-cycles the panel: it keeps its rails and whatever state
  it was in when the DSI stream stopped. There is no panel power switch on this board, so the reset line is the
  only lever, hence the long assertion.
- A failed bring-up holds reset 1000 ms and reboots, at most 3 times in a row (a counter in `RTC_NOINIT` with a
  magic value), then gives up with the log readable.
