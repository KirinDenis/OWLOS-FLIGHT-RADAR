# MQ135: air quality gas sensor

A heated tin-oxide sensor whose output voltage rises with gas (ammonia, benzene, smoke and others). On the OWLOS Air Quality
board on the ADS1015's AIN1 (`ads1015.md`). From `code/aq_sensor_hw.c` and `core/aq.c`; the board in `../air-quality.md`.

How sure: the voltage read on all boards; why it is so low is open. [measured]

## What was found

- About 0.1 V, low, unexplained. [measured]
- Without a calibration gas there are no ppm in it, so none are shown.

## How it is shown: against the cleanest air of the day

- Baseline: the lowest minute of the last 24 hours, after its 3 minute warm-up (`MQ_WARM_S 180`: the heaters start with the
  board). A single quiet minute is real air too: the window was open. Under 10 minutes of history, the lowest since warm-up.
- Level by ratio to baseline: under 1.3 GOOD, under 1.8 FAIR, under 3.0 POOR, above BAD.
- It counts in `air`, the worst of the noses that can be trusted now.

## Power

The MQ heaters are suspected of starving the supply. Not proven: brown-outs are counted in RTC memory and shown, cause open.

## Traps

- Voltages near zero: unexplained. No ppm, a ratio to the day's cleanest minute. [measured, cause open]
