# Waveshare RPi LCD (A) 3.5" on the OWLOS Air Quality board (build `ili9486`)

A Raspberry Pi 3.5" LCD plugged onto the OWLOSAirQuality board, a classic ESP32 board with air sensors. There are 7
such boards.

- Build: `ili9486`, `RADAR_DISPLAY` 3 (`DISPLAY_ILI9486_480x320`), target esp32. The board alone runs OWLOS Air Quality
  UI, which has no number.
- How sure: the panel on these pins is measured under Air Quality UI (seen on the glass). board.h in both radars still
  says "NOT run on hardware yet", and no note says the radar builds ran on it: the radar build is built.

## Chip, flash, USB

ESP32-D0WDQ6 rev 1.0, 4 MB, no PSRAM (measured). USB: a CP210x to UART0 (measured). [esp32](../chips/esp32.md).

## Screen

ILI9486 480x320 behind 74HC shift registers that turn SPI into the panel's 16-bit bus. Every command and parameter
byte goes out as a 16-bit word (the real byte first, then 0x00) with its own CS pulse, CS driven by hand; pixels in
one burst. esp_lcd with 8-bit commands leaves this panel grey. 20 MHz is the most the shift registers take (the old
firmware ran 10). MADCTL 0x28, inversion off, pixel format 0x55 (0x66 gives wrong colours on it). The backlight is
wired to power: no dimming. A whole frame is 123 ms on the wire (measured). Panel:
../../../esp32-lcd-touch/references/panels/ili9486.md.

## Touch

XPT2046 on the same bus, CS 17, at 2 MHz. Air Quality UI drives it with a 9 point quadratic alignment, worst cross 8 px
(measured). The radars do not know of it: their board.h says no touch, BOOT only, and the site sends the rest (on the
radar BOOT steps through the planes, held it changes the range). Old firmware: `TOUCH_CS 17` in
OWLOSAirQuality/OWLOS/lib/TFT_eSPI/User_Setup.h. Its CS floats under the radar (left as it is).

## Buttons and sensors

- BOOT 0.
- Sensors on I2C SDA 21, SCL 22, about 90 kHz (measured unless said):
  - CCS811 at 0x5A, works only when STATUS is read first; it stretches the clock.
  - ADS1015 at 0x48, 12 bit, not the ADS1115 the code names: channel 1 MQ135, 2 MQ7, 3 light.
  - TI HDC1080 at 0x40, IDs 5449/1050, not the SI7021 the README says. Reads about 4.5 C hot, probably board heat.
  - DHT22 on GPIO 32.
  - BMP280 silent at every address on all 7 boards. A module on the same I2C is picked up.
  - The XPT2046's VBAT reads 0 V, unwired.

## Pins

LCD SCLK 18, MOSI 23, MISO 19, DC 2, CS 15 (by hand), RST 4. Touch CS 17. I2C 21, 22. DHT 32.

## board.h

```c
#elif RADAR_DISPLAY == DISPLAY_ILI9486_480x320
/* Waveshare RPi LCD (A) 3.5" on the OWLOSAirQuality board, ViewOwl's ILI9486 build.
 * NOT run on hardware yet. no touch wired that ViewOwl knows of, BOOT button only */
#define DISP_ILI9486   1
#define PIN_LCD_SCLK  18
#define PIN_LCD_MOSI  23
#define PIN_LCD_MISO  19
#define PIN_LCD_DC     2
#define PIN_LCD_CS    15        // driven by hand, CS has to go up after every padded byte
#define PIN_LCD_RST    4
#define LCD_SPI_HZ    (20 * 1000 * 1000)   // the 74HC shift registers on the board top out here
#define LCD_MADCTL    0x28      // MV|BGR, ViewOwl's sweep found this one
#define DISP_STRIP    8         // low, the map's picture needs the heap. the 3.5" went to 6 with the weather: not run here with it yet
#define PIN_BUTTON     0
```

Air Quality UI's board.h: the LCD as here, plus `PIN_T_CS 17`, `TOUCH_RAW_SPAN 3700`, I2C SDA 21, SCL 22 at
`I2C_HZ 100000`, `PIN_DHT 32`, `DISP_STRIP 8`.

## sdkconfig and partitions

- Flight Radar: as [ili9341](ili9341.md), strips of 8, no PWM.
- Air Quality UI: `sdkconfig.defaults` with CPU 240, flash DIO 40, `CONFIG_ESP_BROWNOUT_DET=y`; app 0x200000, `aqhist`
  at 0x210000 size 0x80000.

## Measured (Air Quality UI)

Only changed column runs are sent and two cores draw: frame about 110 ms, about 8 fps, a still screen sends nothing.

## Traps

- ESP-IDF 5.5's `i2c_master` spun forever after a NACK here, so Air Quality UI bit-bangs I2C (firmware/main/i2c_bus.c)
  with an RTC-memory guard that skips a sensor that hung the bus until power off. Which NACK is not recorded.
- The ILI9486 stays grey on esp_lcd's 8-bit commands: pad every byte to 16 bits with its own CS pulse.
